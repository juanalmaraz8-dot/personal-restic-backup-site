# Privacy Policy — Personal Restic Backup

**Last updated:** November 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

Personal Restic Backup is a personal-use application that uses Restic and Rclone to store encrypted computer backups in the application owner's Google Drive account.

The application is not offered as a public service.

## 2. Google Drive Access

The application requests the Google Drive `drive.file` OAuth permission.

This permission allows the application to create and manage files in Google Drive that the application is authorized to access.

The application uses this access solely to store, retrieve, and manage the owner's encrypted backups.

## 3. Data Protection

Restic encrypts backup contents locally before they are transferred to Google Drive.

Backup passwords are managed separately from Google authentication credentials.

Google OAuth tokens are stored locally in Rclone's configuration and are not published on this website.

## 4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or intentionally share backup contents with third parties.

Encrypted backup data is stored with Google Drive as the selected storage provider.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files remain in Google Drive until the application owner removes them.

The owner controls backup retention and may revoke the application's Google account access through Google's security settings.

## 6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application administrator using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
